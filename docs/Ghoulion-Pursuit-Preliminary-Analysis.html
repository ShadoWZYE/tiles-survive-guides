TILES SURVIVE - GHOULION PURSUIT

What the rally reports actually show

Preliminary analysis of 10 Ghoulion rallies from State #1007 on 8 September 2026, checked against the current client configuration.

Short answer

The displayed Ghoulion result is calculated as the exact sum of each member's independently displayed damage. Identical participant damage repeats under different rally leaders. In this sample, changing the leader did not change another player's contribution.

This supports using a weak march to open one rally while stronger marches manually join other rallies—provided every march gets into a Ghoulion rally and no strong march is left idle.

Confirmed calculation

Rally Damage Caused = member 1 damage + member 2 damage + ... + member N damage

The equality holds exactly, to the point, in every supplied report. The top number is not a separate leader calculation or an opaque multiplier.

Unexplained calculation fingerprint

All 49 visible member contributions are exactly one or two points below a multiple of 200: 38 values have remainder 198 and 11 have remainder 199 when divided by 200.

This strongly indicates quantization or rounding inside the combat calculation. The client records show that the level-1 Ghoulion uses a special tier-10 boss infantry unit with health value 1, five level-10 NPC heroes, and a flat all-hero defense value of 9,878. Those records do not explain which operation creates the 200-point steps, so the pattern is reported as observed—not mislabeled as a decoded formula.

Recommended 30-minute playbook

  1. R4/R5 raises the Beast Trap donation buff before starting the event.
  2. Players open one Ghoulion rally with their weakest usable march.
  3. They manually place their strongest remaining marches into other Ghoulion rallies.
  4. Fill rallies and keep all marches cycling. Do not leave a strong march idle merely to preserve the pattern.
  5. Do not start unrelated rallies during the event window.
  6. If too few Ghoulion rallies exist, start another immediately—even with a strong squad. Throughput matters more than ritual.

What these reports cannot tell us

The summary reports do not contain heroes, troop counts, gear, Behemoths or per-squad attributes. Therefore they cannot identify which formation produced a damage tier or reconstruct the server's low-level troop-versus-boss equation.

The extended damage section below the summary repeats or expands damage attribution; it does not expose the missing formation inputs.

The client contains attribute aggregation and reward tables, but the authoritative battle result arrives from the server. Any exact hero damage equation published without controlled formation inputs would currently be guesswork.
